High-fidelity concept-stage desktop homepage UI mockup for the Spanish digital consultancy satorus, 16:10 landscape, straight-on browser viewport, no device frame. Functional website structure: slim navigation, clear hero hierarchy, primary button and beginning of the next section. Audience: Spanish small-business owners without programming knowledge. Offer: useful websites and automations explained without jargon.

Committed art direction “El taller en movimiento”: a contemporary working studio where familiar small-business objects become raw material and a visible route turns messy work into an orderly flow. Editorial, asymmetric, energetic, tactile and human. Palette at page scale: tool cobalt #173BFF, action orange #FF5A35, label lime #E9FF54, graphite #101411 and cool work-paper #F3F5EE. Expressive compact grotesque headline, highly legible humanist sans body. Physical materials: powder-coated metal, paper slips, orange cable, graphite marks, phone, keys, order pad and label roll. Depth from real object shadows. One continuous orange cable connects the scene and ends at the CTA.

Render clearly: “satorus.”, “Tu negocio, menos enredado.”, “Webs y automatizaciones para pymes, explicadas sin tecnicismos.” and “Cuéntame qué te frena”. No cards, bento grid, metrics, pricing, dashboard, gradients, glass, generic AI imagery, robots, neon, cream-and-terracotta consulting style, fake clients, testimonials or watermark.

Composition A — Split workbench. Left 43% is one large cobalt typographic field with brand and headline. Right 57% is an overhead workbench scene with phone, order pad, keys, label roll and one hand connecting the orange cable. Cable crosses from objects into UI and ends at the orange CTA near lower left. Hero occupies about 78% of viewport; beginning of next act is a full-width horizontal working scene, never cards. Strong edge crops and controlled overlap.
